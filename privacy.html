Section 01

1. Introduction

Welcome to SELFCARE DIAGNOSTICS ("we", "our", "us"). We provide clinical diagnostic sample collection services, including physical walk-in desk intake and home sample collection visits by trained phlebotomists across Chennai and neighbouring localities in Tamil Nadu, India.

We recognise that clinical tests, doctor prescriptions, and laboratory reports represent some of your most sensitive personal information. We are committed to handling every specimen, booking record, communication, and diagnostic result with strict professional confidentiality, integrity, and ethical care.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, transfer, retain, and safeguard personal data when you interact with our website (https://selfcarediagnostics.in), schedule home collections, communicate via phone or WhatsApp, or receive digital test reports from us.

Regulatory Framework: This policy is drafted in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and with structural alignment to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and its applicable implementing rules as notified by the Government of India.
Section 02

2. About SELFCARE DIAGNOSTICS

SELFCARE DIAGNOSTICS operates as a diagnostic sample collection centre and healthcare logistics facilitator with its operational base in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

  • Business Structure: Diagnostic sample collection service provider with doorstep phlebotomy coordination.
  • Physical Operations: Sample reception counter and phlebotomy team dispatch based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Operational Scope: Intake of booking requests, doorstep collection of biological specimens (blood, urine, etc.) by trained technicians, secure specimen transportation, delivery to authorized analytical testing laboratories, and prompt electronic delivery of certified patient reports.

Transparency Note: SELFCARE DIAGNOSTICS operates primary sample collection and logistics facilities. Analytical sample processing, clinical pathology examinations, and specialized testing are performed in collaboration with certified, quality-compliant clinical laboratories.

Section 04

4. How We Use Your Information

Your personal and clinical details are processed strictly for defined, lawful healthcare service purposes:

  • Booking Administration: Confirming appointments, managing time slots, assigning phlebotomists, and maintaining counter logs.
  • Doorstep Phlebotomy Coordination: Enabling authorized phlebotomists to locate your premises, verify patient identity, and record pre-test vitals or fasting criteria.
  • Specimen Logistics & Laboratory Processing: Affixing unique sample tracking numbers (barcodes) and transferring biological specimens to accredited partner analytical laboratories.
  • Diagnostic Report Delivery: Generating and delivering password-protected or link-verified laboratory report PDFs to your registered email and WhatsApp.
  • Invoicing & Transaction Reconciliation: Generating digital diagnostic receipts, managing refunds (if applicable), and complying with statutory tax bookkeeping.
  • Patient Care & Support: Answering enquiries regarding test preparation (e.g., 10-12 hours fasting for Lipid Profile or FBS), resolving report access issues, and addressing feedback.
  • Service & Quality Improvement: Auditing technician arrival punctuality, optimizing route efficiency in Chennai, and ensuring high laboratory turnaround time compliance.

No Unrelated Secondary Use: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade patient personal information or clinical records to data brokers, pharmaceutical advertisers, or insurance telemarketers.

Section 06

6. Medical Information & Health Data Confidentiality

We treat all health-related data—including prescriptions, ordered test names, specimen details, and pathological findings—as strictly confidential Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) under Indian law.

  • Role-Based Access: Only technicians assigned to your home visit and clinical lab personnel processing your specimen have access to operational and test parameters.
  • Sample Anonymization: Biological specimens are labelled with unique alphanumeric specimen/barcode identifiers to limit exposure of patient demographic details during transit.
  • Ethical Duty: Our staff and technicians are bound by strict contractual and operational non-disclosure obligations. Breaches of patient medical confidentiality result in immediate termination and applicable legal proceedings.
Section 07

7. Sharing Information with Authorized Third Parties

We do not disclose patient information to third parties except as strictly necessary to fulfill the diagnostic and healthcare services you requested:

  • Partner Clinical Laboratories: To perform analytical processing, chemical pathology, microbiology, or haematology evaluations on your collected specimens.
  • Authorized Phlebotomists & Field Technicians: To reach your residential or workplace address, collect biological samples, and verify patient identity.
  • Payment Processing Gateways: To securely process online UPI, debit/credit card, and net-banking transactions. Only billing references and transaction amounts are shared.
  • Cloud & Infrastructure Providers: For hosting our secure web portal, databases, and secure document storage under enterprise-grade confidentiality agreements.
  • Communications Service Providers: Regulated telecom SMS gateways and Meta/WhatsApp API partners to deliver transactional notifications and report links.
  • Statutory & Law Enforcement Authorities: When strictly mandated under applicable Indian law, court summons, public health surveillance mandates (e.g., notifiable infectious diseases under the Epidemic Diseases Act / Public Health regulations), or government authority orders.

8. Data Storage & Technical Security

We implement reasonable physical, administrative, and technical security practices to safeguard personal and diagnostic data against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Technical Safeguards

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption across our web domain.
  • Access-controlled database permissions.
  • Isolated digital storage for completed PDF reports.
  • Automated server firewalls and vulnerability monitoring.

Operational Safeguards

  • Physical security at our Chennai collection desk.
  • Sealed, temperature-regulated cold-chain transport for biological specimens.
  • Regular review of technician access privileges.
  • Periodic personnel refresher training on data handling.
Realistic Security Disclaimer: While we enforce robust technical and operational controls in compliance with applicable standards, no method of electronic transmission over the internet or method of digital storage can be guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable. Consequently, we cannot warrant absolute data security against criminal cyber-attacks or platform-level vulnerabilities beyond our reasonable control.

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies and browser local storage technologies solely to ensure seamless web portal operation:

✏️

Section 13

13. Children’s & Minors’ Privacy

Diagnostic tests (such as paediatric blood counts, neonatal jaundice screens, or routine child health checks) are frequently performed for minors. We take special precautions to protect the privacy and wellbeing of children:

  • We collect minor patient details (Name, Age, DOB) only with the explicit consent and authorization of a parent, legal guardian, or authorized adult caregiver.
  • Doorstep sample collection for any child below 18 years of age is performed strictly in the presence and supervision of a parent or guardian.
  • We do not market diagnostic services directly to children or knowingly permit minors to register independently on our web application.
